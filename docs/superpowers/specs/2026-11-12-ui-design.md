# Elivo Payout Guard — UI Design

Date: 2026-11-12 · Status: draft (founder skipped questions; designer's calls throughout) · Applies to `apps/web`

> Testnet prototype. Payout firms and SPEI receipts are simulated. Contract unaudited.

## Intent

Judges see the web app in a 3-minute video. The screens have to make one story readable at a glance: **a firm fails, the payout is rerouted, the bond pays the DAO, and a signed receipt proves it.** The current UI works but looks like a default admin page.

The founder pointed at oravio.io as the reference. We take its **visual language**, not its brand:

- cream content sheets on a deep navy page;
- lavender and peach accents;
- chunky display headlines with small monospace eyebrow labels;
- tilted "sticker" cards with thick ink outlines and hard offset shadows;
- pill buttons;
- ticket-shaped stat cards.

**Anti-goals:**
- Anything that copies Oravio's name, logo, copy, illustrations or exact assets.
- Anything that makes the prototype look like a live money service. The testnet banner stays first on every page.
- Losing a test-pinned string. Every label the tests assert stays word-for-word.

**Type** (self-hosted with `@fontsource`, so no network font fetch at build):
- display: Unbounded variable, 700–800;
- body: DM Sans variable;
- labels: JetBrains Mono 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em.

## Shell

- **Testnet banner:** a full-width peach band with ink text in mono uppercase. It is always first.
- **Page:** ink background with a faint pattern of broken rings (inline SVG data URI, 6% opacity, generic).
- **Nav:** a floating pill bar with a cream background and ink outline. Brand on the left ("Elivo Payout Guard", with a small two-arc glyph of our own), links in the middle (New batch · Firms), and a network chip on the right (`SANDBOX` / `TESTNET`).
- **Content:** a cream "sheet" with a 28px radius, max-width 1080px and 16px gutters on phones.

## Primitives (`apps/web/components/ui.tsx`)

- **`Eyebrow`:** mono label, e.g. `01 · UPLOAD`.
- **`Sticker`:** a card with a 2px ink border, radius 16, hard shadow, and an optional `tilt` of −2°…2° (disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **`Button`** (`primary` peach / `secondary` outline / `dark` ink): a pill with a 2px border and hard shadow. It presses down 2px on `:active`.
- **`Chip`:** a small pill with a state colour (mint, peach, lavender, rose, paper).
- **`Ticket`:** a stat card with notched sides (radial-gradient mask), a mono label, and a display-font value.
- **`Steps`:** the four-step progress bar `01 Upload · 02 Quote · 03 Vote · 04 Status`, showing which step is current and which are done.

## Screens

1. **Home (`/`):**
   - **Hero sheet:** eyebrow `TESTNET · NEAR · SPUTNIK DAO`, H1 "Payouts a DAO can check.", the current subtitle copy, and buttons "Start a batch" and "See a finished batch". The second goes to `/chain/5` on testnet and `/new` on sandbox.
   - **Corner stickers:** four tilted stickers sit around the hero and tell the story, each one a real mechanic:
     - "MX-3 missed its cut-off" → "Rerouted to MX-2";
     - "Bond paid 0.20 USDC to the DAO";
     - "Account closed → refunded, no penalty";
     - "Receipt hash anchored ✓".

     On phones they stack under the hero.
   - **Ticket row** (real contract parameters): `1 BP / PERIOD LATE`, `2% CAP`, `BOND × 20 EXPOSURE`.
   - **How it works:** a lavender band with four numbered stickers (01 Upload, 02 DAO vote, 03 Delivery & reroute, 04 Signed receipt).
2. **New batch (`/new`):**
   - `Steps` at step 1, then an upload sticker with a dashed drop area, the sample buttons as pills, and the paste box.
   - Errors appear as a rose sticker listing `Row 5: clabe_check_digit`.
   - On success: the payee table in a sticker, then three tickets (Payouts, Fee buffer (25 bps), Total to lock); rate and timing as mono chips; the pay-in quote as a lavender sticker with asset pills; and "Continue to proposal" as a primary button.
3. **Batch (`/batches/[id]`):**
   - `Steps` shows Vote, or Status once funded.
   - The DAO vote card is a lavender sticker, with the demo-signer label as a chip.
   - **Status:** a ticket row (Locked, Delivered, Refunded to DAO, Penalties to DAO), then payout rows as cards on mobile and a table on desktop:
     - the state is a coloured chip;
     - the route is chips joined by → with the late firm in rose;
     - the penalty is a peach chip when non-zero.
   - **Receipt card:** a dark ink sticker with the mono hash, a Download button and the verify command in a mono block.
4. **Chain (`/chain/[id]`):** the same status block, read-only.
5. **Firms (`/firms`):** three ticket cards, one per firm, each showing the on-time %, the penalties paid, the volume, and a bond bar against max exposure.

## Behaviour and constraints

- All strings that tests assert stay unchanged; new copy is added around them.
- No new network calls. Layout only.
- Contrast is AA on every text and background pair, there is a visible focus ring (`--violet`), and tilt and press animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- There is no dark-mode variant: the design is already dark outside the sheets. `color-scheme: light` is set on the sheets.
